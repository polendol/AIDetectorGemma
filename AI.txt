The evolution of VLS research reflects a transition from descriptive inventories to validated frameworks, basically those of Schmitt and Zhang. These models are anchored in the functional distinction between "discovery" strategies for initial decoding and "consolidation" strategies for long-term retention. While Schmitt highlights a developmental shift from mechanical to deep processing, Zhang underscores the regulatory power of metacognition.
However, these foundational taxonomies are primarily rooted in traditional classroom or reading contexts. At the FE stage involving AV input, learners encounter a significantly higher cognitive load due to high-speed, multi-sensory stimuli. This needs an examination of how strategic behaviors manifest during the complex orchestration of sound, imagery, and on-screen text, as, for instance, reliance on external tools, such as dictionaries, will shift toward real-time, online processing strategies.
